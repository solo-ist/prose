Changed from the live listing: `distraction free` and `free` out; `GPT`, `OpenAI`, `DeepSeek`, `track changes` in.

### What's New in 1.10.0

Any model, and every change has a name on it.

* Three providers, one list. Add an OpenAI key or an Inference Cooperative key beside your Anthropic one and pick Claude, GPT or open-weight models (DeepSeek, GLM, Qwen and others) from the same menu. One key per provider, kept in the Keychain.
* A default model per mode. Give Chat, Editor and Create each a model, and switching mode switches to it.
* Suggesting mode. Click the pen and your own edits become suggestions: insertions underlined, deletions struck through. The file keeps the original until you accept.
* Who did what. Every comment, reply, suggestion and AI edit names its author: you, or Prose with the model that wrote it. Activity shows each suggestion's history and filters by author.
* Switch model from the keyboard with ⇧⌘M.

### Description — the sections that change

The rest of the description stays as it is in `docs/app-store-copy.md`.

**Review and Track Every Suggestion** (last two paragraphs replaced)

Regardless of the AI Mode, you stay in control when AI touches the page.

AI edits arrive as inline comments and suggestions, never silent rewrites. Accept or reject each change individually, leave feedback, or sweep through everything in Quick Review or a full side-by-side diff.

Every comment, suggestion and AI edit names its author: you, or Prose with the model that wrote it. Prose keeps a history of each suggestion and highlights non-human words with a clean, clear visual cue, so you can keep track and stay in charge.

Your document stays clean until you say so.

**Suggest Your Own Changes** (new, after the section above)

Turn on Suggesting mode and your own edits become proposals too. What you type is underlined, what you delete is struck through, and the file on disk keeps the original until you accept. Your changes and the AI's wait in the same review.

**Bring Your Own Key** (replaces the current section)

Your conversations are yours. Use your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or Inference Cooperative key, or all three, and pick a Claude, GPT or open-weight model from one short list. Set a default model for each mode and switch whenever you like. Prose sends your messages straight to the provider you choose, with nothing of ours in between: no data capture, no training on your data, no markup, no subscription.

### App Privacy note (last line replaced)

Note: when you use AI features, Prose passes your API key and message content directly to the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI or the Inference Cooperative). See that provider's privacy policy for details.

* "Edits History" is called **Activity** in the app; the slide below uses the app's name.

### Prompt 1 — the set (paste first)

```text
You are designing the Mac App Store screenshot slides for Prose 1.10.0. Use the Prose Design System for every colour, type and spacing decision; do not introduce anything it doesn't define.

What Prose is: a free, open-source markdown editor for the Mac with optional AI. Its position is "AI that doesn't write for you": the AI comments and suggests, and the writer accepts or rejects every change. People bring their own API key. Files are plain .md and .txt on disk.

Who reads these slides: writers and developers browsing the App Store who are wary of AI writing tools. They decide in a few seconds from the first two or three slides. The tone is plain, confident and a little opinionated. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

What is new in 1.10.0, and the reason for this refresh:
1. Three model providers in one menu: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT) and the Inference Cooperative (open-weight models such as DeepSeek, GLM and Qwen). One key per provider. The menu lists each provider's flagship models, and each mode (Chat, Editor, Create) can have its own default model.
2. Suggestion mode: the writer's own edits become tracked changes (insertions underlined, deletions struck through) and the file keeps the original until they accept.
3. Authorship: every comment, reply, suggestion and AI edit names who made it, the writer or "Prose" with the model.

Deliverable: seven artboards at 2560 × 1600 px (16:10, the App Store's Mac size), one per slide, in the order of the table I give you next. Each slide has one headline, one sub line and one app capture. The capture is the subject; the copy sits above or beside it and must stay readable at thumbnail size in the App Store listing. Slides alternate light and dark as listed. All seven must read as one set: same grid, same type scale, same capture treatment.

Captures: I will attach a real app capture for each slide. Use it as is; do not redraw or restyle the app's interface. Where a capture is missing, put a clearly labelled placeholder block of the same size and write "CAPTURE NEEDED" on it with the slide's scene name. Never invent interface.

Hard rules:
- This is the App Store edition. Never mention or show MCP, remote access, sharing, share links, ChatGPT or claude.ai connectors, Claude Desktop or reMarkable.
- Do not show Apple hardware frames, App Store badges, prices, ratings or other platforms.
- Never mention membership, joining, pricing or sign-up for any provider.
- Model and company names appear only as plain text, the way the app shows them. No Anthropic, OpenAI or Inference Cooperative logos.
- Use the exact headline and sub I give for each slide unless I ask for variants.

Before you draw all seven, show me slide 1 and slide 4 so I can approve the layout in light and in dark. Then build the rest.
```
