## M3 (#1237): MCP targeting and manners

From Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP client connected to the dev build, with two documents open in Prose.

- [ ] Ask the client to `list_tabs`, then to comment on the tab you are **not** looking at (it passes that tab's `document`). The comment lands there; your tab stays in front.
- [ ] While you type in the chat panel, have the client comment on the document in front of you. The comment appears; your text, caret and focus stay in the chat input.
- [ ] With your caret mid-sentence in the editor, have the client suggest an edit to another paragraph. The suggestion appears; your caret doesn't move.
- [ ] Ask the client to comment on a file that isn't open. It gets "No open document matches…" and no tab opens. `open_file` still opens it, and switches to it, as before.
